About the lab

Connecting quantum science to real problems.

We are the Chase Quantum Lab at the University of Illinois Chicago. Our research spans quantum chemistry, quantum computing and algorithms, quantum and AI, quantum for energy, and the materials, photonic devices, and transduction technologies that quantum systems depend on.

We combine computational design, algorithm development, spectroscopy, and experimental validation to connect discoveries to practical quantum technologies.
