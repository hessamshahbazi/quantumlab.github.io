Mission

Materials that make quantum light useful.

The lab combines computational design, device fabrication concepts, spectroscopy, and experimental validation to study light-matter interactions in low-dimensional materials. Our aim is to connect material discovery to quantum photonic components for communication, sensing, networking, and information processing.

Research

Low-dimensional materials for quantum technologies.

01

1D and 2D Quantum Materials

Develop and characterize carbon nanotubes, topological insulators, and atomically thin platforms with optical and electronic properties suited for quantum applications.

02

Quantum Photonic Devices

Design device concepts for single-photon emitters, entangled photon sources, resonant nanophotonic structures, and light-matter coupling in compact materials systems.

03

Quantum Communication and Networks

Translate materials and photonic structures toward components that support advanced quantum communication, networked quantum systems, and scalable quantum hardware.

04

Computation and Validation

Use computational tools to guide materials and device design, then validate promising mechanisms through spectroscopy, photonics measurements, and experimental collaboration.
