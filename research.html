Overview

A broad program in quantum science and technology.

The lab combines computational design, algorithm development, spectroscopy, and experimental validation to study quantum phenomena in molecules and materials, and to build the devices and algorithms that use them. Our aim is to connect fundamental discoveries to practical technologies for chemistry, energy, computing, sensing, and communication.
